1. Overview

Silo Launcher ("Silo", "we", "our", or "us") is a minimal, context-shifting Android launcher. Our core promise is simple: your data belongs to you. All app management, context rules, and settings run locally on your phone without sending personal information to remote servers.

2. How App Capabilities Are Used

Silo Launcher requests device capabilities only to deliver core launcher features:

  • App Drawer & Launching: Needed to show your installed apps and launch them when tapped.
  • Automatic Mode Switching: Optional rules use location or connected Bluetooth devices to trigger focus profiles (such as Work or Gym mode) entirely in real-time.
  • Do Not Disturb Integration: Adjusts focus and silent modes when you change Silo profiles.
  • Tactile Feedback & Boot Setup: Delivers haptic feedback during interactions and restores your preferences when your phone restarts.

3. Zero Data Selling & Advertising

We do not collect, monetize, rent, or sell your personal data. Silo Launcher contains no third-party ad networks and no behavioral cross-app tracking.

4. Your Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your launcher data at all times:

  • Clear all stored preferences anytime under Android Settings > Apps > Silo > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling Silo Launcher immediately and permanently removes all saved data from your phone.

5. Children's Privacy & Compliance

Silo Launcher is compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, Silo is safe for all age groups.
